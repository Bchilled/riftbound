How to Play — The A B C D E F G Turn Structure

Every turn in Riftbound follows the same 7-phase sequence. Learn this and you'll never be lost.

▶
A

AWAKEN

All exhausted units and legends ready. This is your free reset — every unit is ready to act again.

B

BEGINNING

Score points for held battlefields. If your unit holds a field alone — score 1 point. First to 8 points wins.

C

CHANNEL

Draw runes from your 12-card rune deck. Runes are your energy — you need them to play cards.

D

DRAW

Draw 1 card from your main deck. Some cards and abilities let you draw more.

E

ENGAGE

Play cards from your hand. Units, spells, gear — spend runes to play them to your base or battlefields.

F

FIGHT

Move units from your base to battlefields and declare attacks. Units fight if both sides have units there.

G

GO TO END

Combat resolves. Units that dealt enough damage conquer the field. Conquered fields score points. End your turn.
