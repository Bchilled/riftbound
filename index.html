RIFTBOUND

War Table  ·  Origins & Spiritforged

Choose Your Legend

A

AWAKEN & BEGINNING

All exhausted units and your legend ready up. Then score points for any battlefields your units are holding alone. First to 8 points wins.

B

CHANNEL & DRAW

Draw runes from your 12-card rune deck — these are your energy to play cards. Then draw 1 card from your main deck.

C

PLAY & DEPLOY

Spend runes to play units, spells, and gear. Units go to your base first, then move to battlefields. Reactions can be played any time — even on your opponent's turn.

D

FIGHT & CONQUER

Attack with your units. If yours deal more damage, you conquer the field. Conquered fields score points if you hold them next turn. Pass turn to opponent.
