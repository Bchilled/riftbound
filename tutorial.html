A
Awaken
Untap everything. Every exhausted unit, rune, legend, and gear stands back up. Think of it like your team taking a deep breath before the next round. Everything that was sideways is now upright and ready to use again.

Board State
Check the board and score points. Any battlefield your units held since last turn — that's a point each. Right now. Free. Then look at the board. What does your opponent have? What do you have? Conjure
Draw 2 runes from your 12-card rune deck. These are your mana for the turn — you'll spend them to play cards. If you chose to go second, you draw 3 runes on your very first turn instead of 2. That's your consolation prize for being polite.

Now you have your cards and your runes. This is where you actually play the game. Play units to your base, cast spells, equip gear, trigger your legend. Units enter your base exhausted — they can't move to a battlefield the same turn they're played. Next turn they're ready and they can move.

E·F·G
Execute — Fight — Go
Move your ready units to the battlefields. If nobody's there — easy point. Scream into the void. You are a god.

If they do have units there, you're in a Showdown. Play spells and reactions. Buff your units. Make theirs weaker. Then combat resolves — total Might vs total Might, damage is assigned, the weak fall, the strong conquer. Units heal fully after combat.

You can also move units back to your base if they're not exhausted — retreat is valid. You can play units directly to a battlefield you already control. And there are hidden cards, counter-spells, equipment, buffs, blinks — the game has layers. But the bones are simple.

There are abilities everywhere. Hidden cards sitting facedown. Reactions that trigger on your opponent's turn. Units that buff on arrival. Gear that changes might mid-combat. You are not always safe — not at your battlefield, not at your base, not even in your hand. That's ok. Neither are they.

The Final Point Rule

You need 8 points to win. But you can't sneak your last point from just one conquest.

Your 8th point has to come from one of these:

Hold it. If you're sitting at 7 points and you hold a battlefield into your B step next turn — that's point 8. You win.

Take both. Conquer battlefield 1 and battlefield 2 in the same turn. You win.

Card effect. Some cards give you points directly. Those can be your last point regardless of battlefield state.

If you try to conquer just one battlefield for your 8th point without holding anything — you draw a card instead. The game continues. They just got a second chance and it's your fault.

Before You Start — Game Setup
Roll
Roll to see who goes first. Most people choose first. Going second gets you 3 runes on turn 1 instead of 2. Choose wisely.
Shuffle & Cut
Shuffle your deck. Offer your opponent a cut. They shuffle theirs. Now you both have no excuses.
Draw 4
Draw your opening hand of 4 cards. Look at them. Don't make a face.
Mulligan
You can put up to 2 cards randomly to the bottom of your deck and draw that many back. One chance. Use it.
Reveal Battlefields
Reveal your 3 pre-chosen battlefields. Pick which 2 you're playing this game. Your opponent does the same. Place them between you.
Play
Legends are revealed. Take turns. First player starts with their A step. Now you're playing Riftbound.
